# Ten years of open data

Title: Ten years of open data
Subtitle: How a city opened its records to everyone
Excerpt: The city council published its first open dataset in 2016.
Category: Technology
Reading time: 6

Internal: check the figures with the press office before publishing.

The city council published its first open dataset in 2016: the timetables of
its buses. Ten years later, more than **400 datasets** are online, from bus
timetables to air quality, and the city answers a request for new data in
less than a month.

- 2016: the first dataset, the bus timetables
- 2020: air quality, street by street
- 2026: more than 400 datasets
