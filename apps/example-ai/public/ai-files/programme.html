Library week 2026

From 7 to 11 December the Central Library opens its doors every evening. Entry is free unless the event says otherwise.

Stories for the little ones

Writing your first novel

Wednesday 9 December 2026, from 18:30 to 20:00, online: a talk with the authors of the city's writing school. Tickets: 8 euros.

A night at the library

Friday 11 December 2026, from 20:00 to midnight, at the Central Library: readings and music to close the week.
